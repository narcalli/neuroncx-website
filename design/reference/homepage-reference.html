The problem

A lead-shaped CRM loses the second visit

Most systems run one straight line: contact, opportunity, closed. Real service relationships repeat, and each return arrives as a stranger.

01

The return is a new lead

After the sale, the next message opens a fresh row. Your team starts cold and the customer explains everything again.

02

The half-finished booking vanishes

Someone stops partway. Nothing watches it, because the pipeline only tracks leads heading toward a sale.

03

One person, four records

WhatsApp, a phone call and two branches produce four entries, and nothing holds the relationship between them.

What changes

One home for the whole story

Context

Every interaction joins its episode

Once an episode opens, each call, message, booking and payment attaches to it. Whoever picks it up sees the history first.

Plan

Every episode knows its next step

Each one carries a plan built around a key date, so the system knows what should happen and when. A missed step gets chased.

Identity

Every episode belongs to one customer

Contacts from different channels and branches resolve to one person. Central and local teams see the same customer.

Bot swarm

Small agents, one job each

Each agent reads your rules, acts in your systems, hands over when a decision needs a person, and logs the step against the episode.

WHATSAPP

Appointment booking

Shares location, matches the nearest branch, confirms a slot. A dropped booking waits where it stopped.

PAYMENTS

Collection in chat

A link goes out as soon as the booking is confirmed, then is tracked until paid and matched with the gateway.

VISION

Prescription reading

Every prescribed test is picked out of a photo. Anything unclear goes to a person, the rest moves to pricing.

ADVISORY

Test counselling

Patients ask from a symptom or a doctor's note, and arrive knowing which tests to take and how to prepare.

ADMISSIONS

Application intake

Marksheets, IDs and photos land in the CRM in order, and counsellors call the students who already qualify.

COMPLIANCE

KYC verification

IDs are read, names and dates cross-checked, your rules applied the same way every time. Only real exceptions escalate.

Selection before scoring

Supplier calls, wrong numbers and dropped connections stay out of the pool. Who called decides whether a call is checked at all.

A fair share per agent

A daily cap divided across the agents who took calls. The busiest agent is not the most checked, and a quiet week costs nobody their score.

Any language, your rubric

Calls are transcribed, carried into the scoring language where needed, then scored line by line against your weights.

Proof behind every score

Each score carries the piece of conversation it came from, so a supervisor can check it rather than trust it, and change it if they disagree.
