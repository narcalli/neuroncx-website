How the swarm is organised

The swarm: small bots, each with one job

NeuronCx does not run one large chatbot. It runs a swarm of small bots, each with a single job, all working from the same customer record. When one bot finishes, it passes the customer to the next bot or to your team, so nobody asks the same question twice.

How every bot in the swarm works

Every bot follows the same five steps, whether it's reading a chat or a call recording. So you always know what it did and why.

  2. 1

    Hears

    Understands the request in the customer's own words, or listens to the recording.

  3. 2

    Checks

    Looks at your rules first: locations, services, prices, slots, scoring sheets.

  4. 3

    Acts

    Does the work in your systems: books, charges, files, scores.

  5. 4

    Hands over

    Brings in your team when a decision needs a person.

  6. 5

    Records

    Logs every step, so you can see what happened.

Under the hood

What sits under every bot

The bots are small, but they all stand on the same three layers. That's what lets them work as one team.

Layer 1One shared customer record
Layer 2Work done in your systems
Layer 3Your team stays in charge

One shared customer record

Every bot knows who the customer is, what they asked before and where they are in their journey. That's how one bot passes work to the next without the customer repeating anything.

Work done in your own systems

Bookings, payments, documents and scores land in the tools you already use. This is WhatsApp automation for business that ends with the job done, not just a message sent.

Your team stays in charge

When a decision needs a person, the conversation moves to your team with the full history attached. You decide which questions always go to a person.

Trust

You can see everything the swarm does

Every bot is narrow, logged and bound by the limits you set.

Every step logged

Each run is recorded step by step, so your team can see what happened and where anything stopped.

Unsure reads go to a person

When a bot isn't confident about what it read, it flags the result for someone to check before anything is saved.

You set the limits

You decide which questions always go to your team and what each bot is allowed to do.

Built on official channels

Customer conversations run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

WhatsApp appointment booking and payment, step by step

Two agents handle a WhatsApp booking from the first request to a confirmed payment: Appointment assistance books the slot, and Payment services sends the payment link and confirms it. Each step follows your own locations, doctors, slots, prices and discount rules, and everything is saved to the customer record.
